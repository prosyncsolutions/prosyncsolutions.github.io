About us

A small studio, on purpose

Velluto is a team of four designers and planners based in Florence. We take on a limited number of events each season so that every client works directly with the person designing their day.

We work with trusted local florists, caterers and venues across Tuscany, and we’re happy to work with suppliers you already love.
