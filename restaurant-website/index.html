Our kitchen

One oven, one fire, and whatever the market has that morning.

We built Ember & Olive around a single oak-fired oven. Everything that can be cooked over flame is: flatbreads, whole fish, vegetables that come out smoky and sweet.

The menu is short on purpose. It changes with the season, and the people cooking it know every supplier by name.
