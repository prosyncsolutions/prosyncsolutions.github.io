Questions people ask

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from your member page. Monthly memberships stop at the end of the month. Yearly memberships run to the end of the paid year.

Do I need gardening experience?

No. Volunteers run a short induction on every open day and tools are provided.

Where does the money go?

Water, seeds, compost, tool repairs and insurance. The annual accounts are published to members.

Can my family use my membership?

Household membership covers everyone at one address. Individual membership is for one adult, and children are always welcome with a member.
